## On source framing

The Investopaper article describes NIC Asia as having "provided consistent
dividends to shareholders." The company paid nothing in four of the last five
fiscal years. The statement is defensible across a twenty-year window and
misleading about the present — an example of the exact failure mode this
project addresses, published by a financial news site rather than an
anonymous influencer.
