## Historical rates

In Ashoj 2079 (September 2022) individual fixed deposits paid 12.133%,
institutions 10.133% and remittance deposits 13.133%, with some banks
offering as much as 15% on remittance deposits. Rates were cut to 11% for
individuals from Magh 2079 (January 2023).
